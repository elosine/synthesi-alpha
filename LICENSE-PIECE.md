# The rights in this repo

Two things live here, under two different terms.

## The program — MIT

`engine/` and `tools/` — the workshop that plays the piece and makes a
version of it.

Free to use, copy, change and pass on, under the MIT licence:
[`LICENSE`](LICENSE).

## The piece — all rights reserved

*Sýnthesi Alpha* — © 2026 elosine. All rights reserved.

Everything else in this repo is the piece and its record. It is NOT
under the MIT licence:

- `voices/` — the recording, its map, the scenario, the played
  sequences, the samples
- `versions/` — the kept versions: each one's sound and its picture
- `picture/` — the drawn score
- `docs/` — the plan, the journals, the record of how the piece was
  made

You are welcome to look and to listen.

Any other use of the piece — copying it on, changing it, publishing
it, performing it — needs the composer's yes. Ask at
https://github.com/elosine
