   describes and nobody built. That part is a build, not a port.

   tools/gen_presets.js draws N seeded presets from named effects, each
   dial within its usual range (13 effects have draw ranges);
   tools/build_fx_audition.js lays them out one per brick, 3 s apart,
   with a sheet of every dial. They are tied to the composer score
   page and the bank.
4. *The engine was built to be taken, not rewritten* — a git subtree
   at `electronics/` (TAKE.md), and its plan already says "The
   improviser piece may take only some of the objects" (PLAN.md 35).

   on it) and, for simulation, Reaper with ReaRoute. This repo has
   neither: its piece is a browser page on Web Audio. The effects are
   SuperCollider. Live, the engine's road is "microphones → the
   interface → the engine's hardware inputs ... No DAW" (SEAMS.md 23).

**The two ideas.**

   three body problem, anticipation–reaction — "some version of that
   between me and the electronics". Checked in `decibel_TENOR_2026`

   electronics/sc/performer.scd (states far · approaching · closePass

   and notation. How flocking was made there was not looked at.
   In *Approaching* these ran between simulated players; here one of
   the parties is him, live — which is why the listening engine comes
   first.
2. *The score as more than one conveyor belt.* His image: "the
   graphics are scrolling along and passing my center cursor and then
   sounding." Two things follow from it. (a) The main score gets
   *scrolling behaviours* — "it might stop at a certain point or

   performer", which "might keep scrolling while the main score is

playhead time" (docs/plan/next-session.md §5a), and playheads already
stop, freeze and run backward — the main score's scrolling behaviours

second belt with its own time. Since the mic openings may end up on
